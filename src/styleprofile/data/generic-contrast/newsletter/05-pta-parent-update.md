# Cedar Hill Elementary PTA: October Update for Families

Dear Cedar Hill families,

We are about six weeks into the school year, and the PTA has a good deal to share. Please read on for dates, results, and a few requests.

## Fall Festival recap

Thank you to everyone who came out on October 4. Roughly 400 people attended, and the event raised $6,310 for classroom supplies and the library fund. The cakewalk was, as usual, the hit of the evening, and our fifth-grade volunteers deserve special praise for running the ring toss without a single complaint. A special thank-you to Mr. Alvarez at Alvarez Hardware for donating the pumpkins.

## Upcoming dates

- **October 22:** PTA general meeting, 6:30 p.m., school library. Childcare will be provided in the cafeteria.
- **October 29:** Picture retake day.
- **November 5:** No school (teacher in-service).
- **November 14:** Book Fair opens in the library and runs through the 20th.
- **November 21:** Family Movie Night, 5:30 p.m., gym. Bring blankets.

## Where your money is going

Last year's fundraising paid for new playground mulch, a classroom set of reading tablets for second grade, and the art supplies cart. This year, teachers voted on priorities, and the top requests were a new sound system for the cafeteria stage, additional classroom library books, and a shade structure over the kindergarten sandbox. We will present a full budget at the October 22 meeting, and everyone is welcome to weigh in.

## Volunteers still needed

We are short on help in three areas. First, we need two parents to coordinate the Book Fair schedule. Second, the hospitality committee is looking for people to organize teacher appreciation lunches, one per quarter. Third, we would love a volunteer to manage our social media page, which has been quiet since June. None of these roles requires a big time commitment, and we are happy to pair you with someone experienced.

## Membership

If you have not yet joined the PTA, it is only $10 per family, and every dollar stays at Cedar Hill. You can sign up online or drop a form in the front office. You do not need to attend meetings to be a member, though we do hope you will.

## A note on drop-off

The principal asked us to remind families that the front circle is for quick drop-offs only. Please pull forward, let your child out, and move along. The side lot on Maple Street has plenty of room if you want to walk your child in.

Thank you, as always, for being part of this community. Questions can go to pta@cedarhillpta.example.

With appreciation,
Lorraine Whitfield, PTA President
