Subject: Welcome to the Harbor Street Food Pantry

Hello, and thank you for signing up.

We are genuinely glad you are here. Last month the pantry served 1,186 households, and that would be impossible without people willing to give up a few hours of their week. Below is what you need to know before your first shift.

**Where to go.** We are in the basement of St. Brendan's Church. Use the side entrance on Harbor Street, the green door with the ramp. Someone will be at the table inside to greet you and check you in.

**When to arrive.** Please come fifteen minutes before your shift begins. Distribution days are Tuesday and Friday, with doors opening to clients at 10:00 a.m. Setup shifts start at 8:30, and cleanup ends around 1:00.

**What to wear.** Closed-toe shoes are required, since we move a lot of heavy boxes. Dress in layers: the warehouse side is chilly and the front room gets warm. We will give you an apron and a name tag.

**What you will do.** New volunteers usually start in one of three places:

- *Sorting and stocking:* checking expiration dates and organizing donations onto shelves.
- *Client choice area:* helping neighbors walk through the pantry and select items, much like a small grocery store.
- *Intake desk:* greeting people and handling sign-in. This one is best once you have a few shifts behind you.

You will always be paired with an experienced volunteer, and nobody expects you to know everything on day one.

**A few ground rules.** We treat everyone who walks through our doors with dignity. Please do not ask people why they need food, and avoid comments about what anyone chooses to take. Photographs are not allowed on the floor without permission. If a situation feels difficult or uncomfortable, find the shift lead right away. That is what they are there for.

**Food safety.** Wash your hands when you arrive and after breaks. Tell us if you are feeling ill, and please stay home if you are. We will happily reschedule you.

**Keeping in touch.** You will receive a text reminder the day before each shift. If you cannot make it, reply to the message or call Teresa, our volunteer coordinator, at 555-0142. Giving us even a day's notice helps enormously.

Finally, bring a water bottle and a sense of humor. The best part of this work is the people you meet, both clients and fellow volunteers, and many of our longest-serving helpers started exactly where you are now.

Thank you for stepping up. We will see you soon.

Teresa Navarro
Volunteer Coordinator, Harbor Street Food Pantry
