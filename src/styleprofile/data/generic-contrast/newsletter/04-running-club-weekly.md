## This week's schedule

- **Tuesday, 6:00 p.m.:** Track workout at Hollis High. We are doing 6 x 800 meters at roughly 10K effort, with a two-minute jog between each. Newer runners can cut it to four repeats, and no one will think less of you.

## Training tip of the week

As the weather cools, give yourself a few extra minutes of warm-up. Cold muscles are stiffer, and a short walk followed by some leg swings and gentle strides can make the first mile feel far less like a chore. Layer up too: you should feel slightly chilly when you start, because you will warm up quickly.
