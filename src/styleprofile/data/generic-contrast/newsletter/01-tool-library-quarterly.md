# Eastgate Tool Library: Fall Quarter Update

Another busy quarter at the shed on Larkin Street. Here is where things stand.

**By the numbers.** Members borrowed 1,412 tools between July and September, up from 1,060 last quarter. The most requested items were the pressure washer, the tile saw, and, somewhat surprisingly, the pasta roller we picked up at a estate sale. We now have 386 active members.

**New arrivals.** Thanks to a grant from the Millbrook Credit Union and several generous donations, the shelves now include a battery-powered chainsaw, two cordless drills, a wet-dry vacuum, a set of sawhorses, and a manual log splitter. All of them are ready to borrow with a quick safety check at the desk.

**Repair Saturdays.** Our monthly repair sessions drew around twenty people each time. Volunteers mended three lawn mowers, sharpened more than sixty pairs of garden shears, and rewired a lamp that, we are told, belonged to someone's grandmother. Next session is October 26, from 10 a.m. to 1 p.m.

**Fall hours.** Beginning November 1 we will open Tuesday and Thursday evenings from 5 to 8, and Saturdays from 9 to 3. We will be closed on Thanksgiving weekend.

**Help wanted.** We need two more people to staff Saturday mornings and someone who enjoys sorting small hardware into labeled bins. No experience is required, only patience.

**A reminder on returns.** Please clean tools before bringing them back, and tell us about anything that broke. We would much rather fix something than guess what went wrong.

See you at the desk,
Marguerite Okafor, Volunteer Coordinator
