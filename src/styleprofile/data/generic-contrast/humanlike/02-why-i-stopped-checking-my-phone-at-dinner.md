Honestly, nothing dramatic made me do it. No wake-up call, no lecture from a wise friend.

It was a Tuesday. My partner was telling me a story about a coworker and a stuck elevator, and I was nodding in all the right places while reading an article about, I think, the history of paper clips. When the story ended she paused, waiting for me to laugh, and I had no idea where to laugh. I'd missed the whole thing. She didn't say anything. She just picked up her fork again, and that small quiet was worse than any argument we could have had.

So now the phone lives in a drawer by the front door when we sit down to eat. That's it. That's the whole system.

The first week was twitchy. My hand kept drifting toward a pocket that was empty, like a phantom limb situation. I'd wonder what I was missing, and the answer, every single time, was nothing. Newsletters. A sale on socks.

What I got back is hard to explain without sounding corny. Dinners got longer. I started hearing the endings of stories. Last night she told me the elevator one again, just to see if I'd laugh this time, and I did, really hard.
